> **Review this before launch.** It describes what the site actually does today,
> but it is not legal advice.

## What this site stores on your device

One thing, and only if you use the feature: the stories you bookmark, plus your
light/dark preference. Both live in your own browser's local storage. They never
leave your device and there is no account attached to them. Clearing your browser
data removes them.

There are no advertising cookies and no analytics on this site.

## When you contact me

The contact form collects your name, email address and message, so that I can
reply. Submissions are processed and stored by [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/),
who host this site, and forwarded to my email inbox. They are used to answer you
and nothing else — never sold, never added to a marketing list.

## When you subscribe

If you subscribe for new-story notifications, your email address is used to send
you an email when a story is published. Nothing else. Every email includes an
unsubscribe link.

## Fonts

Typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to
Google when the page loads.

## Getting your data removed

Email [{{ site.author.email }}](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}) and I will delete
anything I hold about you.
