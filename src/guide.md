Bookmark this page. It is not linked from anywhere on the site and search engines
are told to ignore it, so only people you give the address to will find it.

## Publishing a story

1. Go to **[/admin/](/admin/)** on this site.
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub**. You only do this once per device — after that it
   remembers you.
3. On the left, click **Founder Stories**, then the **New Story** button.
4. Fill in the boxes (see below).
5. Press **Publish** at the top.

That is it. The story appears on the site in about a minute. **The web address of the
site never changes** — anyone who has already bookmarked it, or found it on Google,
will see the new story automatically. You never need to send anyone a new link.

## What goes in each box

**Headline** — the title. Aim for 55–65 characters. Brand name first, then the
founder's name. *"HelloCloud Founder Story: How Jalaj Verma Built a Gen Z Skincare
Brand"*

**Dek** — one or two sentences that make someone want to open it. This is also what
shows up underneath the title on Google, so make it count.

**Kicker** — the small line in capitals. Sector, then model, then city:
*Skincare · D2C · Jhansi*

**Publish date** — defaults to today. Change it if you want.

**Cover image** — drag a photo in, or click to choose one. Landscape pictures work
best. This is also the picture that appears when someone shares the link on
WhatsApp or LinkedIn.

**Cover image description** — a few words describing the photo, for readers who use a
screen reader. *"Jalaj Verma in the HelloCloud workshop."*

**Story** — the article itself. The toolbar gives you bold, italic, headings, quotes,
links and images. You can paste straight from Google Docs.

**Save as draft** — turn this **on** to save without publishing. Nobody can see it,
and it has no web address at all. Turn it off when you are ready.

## Things you never have to do

These all work themselves out. Do not go looking for them:

- The **FN number** — counts up on its own in publishing order
- The **reading time** — measured from the length of what you wrote
- The **web address** of the story — made from the headline
- The **"Read the latest story"** button on the front page — always points at the
  newest one
- The **RSS feed**, the **sitemap**, and the **Google information** — all rebuilt
  every time you publish

## Publishing a note

Notes are the shorter pieces in the *Brand & Founder Communication* section.

Click **Field Notes** in the left sidebar instead of Founder Stories. The important
box is **Status**:

- **Writing now** — the note appears on the front page with a small pulsing dot, so
  people can see what is coming. It is deliberately **not** clickable and has no page.
  Use this to announce something before you have written it.
- **Published** — the note becomes clickable and opens on the page, exactly like a
  story does.

So the normal rhythm is: create the note with a title and summary, leave it on
*Writing now*, and switch it to *Published* once you have actually written it.

**Hide completely** removes it from the site altogether, even the "Writing now" line.

## Fixing something you already published

Go to **/admin/**, click the story or note in the list, change what you want, and
press **Publish** again. The site updates the same way. Nothing breaks, and the web
address stays the same.

To take something down entirely, open it and use **Delete** — or, more gently, turn
**Save as draft** back on so you keep the writing.

## Common questions

**Do I need to send people a new link when I publish?**
No. Never. The site address stays the same and new stories appear on it by
themselves.

**What if I make a mistake?**
Everything is saved with a full history. Nothing is ever really lost — ask whoever
set this up and any version can be brought back.

**Can I write on my phone?**
Yes. `/admin/` works on a phone, including adding photos from your camera roll.

**Where do messages from the contact form go?**
Straight to your email inbox, and they are also kept in the site's dashboard as a
backup.

**Something looks wrong and I do not know why.**
Do not try to fix it in a panic — nothing you can click will break the site
permanently. Write down what you did and ask for help.
