I write about how founders build brands, survive failures, and turn ideas into
businesses. Not launch announcements and not press releases — the reasoning
underneath the decisions, reported properly and written down.

## What Field Notes is

Every story here comes out of a real conversation, usually more than one. I ask
founders the questions that do not make it into the pitch deck: what nearly went
wrong, what they got wrong first, and what they would tell someone starting the
same week they did.

## Working together

I help founders turn what is already in their head into assets that build trust
before a single sales call — founder journey articles, brand stories, thought
leadership, and About pages that sound like a person rather than a committee.
